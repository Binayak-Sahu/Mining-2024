                                THE MINES ACTS, 1952
EXAMINATION FOR MANAGER’S, OVERMAN’S, FOREMAN’S, SURVEYOR’S, SIRDAR’S, MATE’S, BLASTER’S
                    AND GAS TESTING CERTIFICATE OF COMPETENCY
                  UNDER THE METALLIFERIOUS MINES REGULATIONS, 1961
                                         &
                          THE COAL MINES REGULATIONS, 2017

The Chairman, Board of Mining Examinations and Director General of Mines Safety, Dhanbad is holding Computer Based
Examinations for Manager’s, Overman’s, Foreman’s, Surveyor’s, Sirdar’s, Mate’s, Blaster’s and Gas Testing Certificate of
Competency under the Metalliferous Mines Regulations, 1961, the Coal Mines Regulations, 2017 and the Mines Act, 1952 in
June, 2025 at Ajmer, Asansol/ Durgapur, Bengaluru, Chennai, Neyveli, Bhilai, Bhubaneswar, Bokaro, Cuttack, Dhanbad,
Ghaziabad & Delhi/ NCR, Goa, Guwahati, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Jamshedpur, Nagpur, Raipur, Bilaspur, Varanasi, Ranchi and
Udaipur centres. Examinations of Sirdar’s, Mate’s, Blaster’s & Gas Testing will be conducted in English, Hindi, Bengali,
Oriya, Telugu, Kannada & Tamil Languages. Candidates who desire to appear in the examination are required to submit their
application ONLINE by using the link provided in the DGMS website. No other mode for submission of application shall be
accepted.

SCHEDULE OF EXAMINATION: The Computer Based Examination is scheduled to be held from 03rd to 07th June, 2025.
Copy of admit card/ authorisation letter shall be made available for applicant to download and applicant are required to download
it and present it for examination.
