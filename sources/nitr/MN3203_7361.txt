Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN3203 : Underground Hazards and Rescue { 3-0-0 / 3}
Subject Nature :
 Theory
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
Introduction: Various types of Mine hazards encountered during mining including Blasting, use of mining machinery, Roof fall, and Side fall. 
Module 2 : 
Coal Mine fire - Classification of mine fire, the genesis of mine fire, Spontaneous heating of coal, incubation period, detection of fire, prevention, and control of mine fire in underground coal mines
Module 3 : 
Mine Explosion – Methane explosion, Coal dust explosion, genesis, detection, prevention, and control in underground coal mines 
Module 4 : 
Water danger in mines, Surface and underground precautions to prevent flooding of the mines, Inundation of mines, and dewatering
Module 5 : 
Rock Bursts, Coal Mine Bumps, detection, prevention, and control technology used in mines 
Module 6 : 
Rescue and Recovery operations in mines, Rescue station, Rescue equipment, classification, recovery operations after mine fire, explosion, and inundation. 
 Course Objective
 1 .
To understand hazards associated with underground coal mining
 2 .
To understand the mechanism of various types of hazards associated with underground coal mining
 3 .
To understand the mitigation measures to be taken to face the various types of hazards in mines
 4 .
To understand the monitoring and control of various types of hazards associated with underground coal mining
 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO 1. The students will learn the process of identifying and assessing different hazards present in underground mines like fire, explosion, roof fall, side fall, rock bursts, coal bumps, inundation etc.
 2 .
CO 2. Acquire comprehensive knowledge on the causes of underground mine disaster and their mitigation procedure.
 3 .
CO 3. The students will gain understanding on mine rescue and recovery procedures.
 4 .
CO 4. Device and implement ‘Standard Operating Procedures’ and ‘Code of Practices’ 
 5 .
CO 5. Development of Emergency Management Plan and its implementation procedure.
