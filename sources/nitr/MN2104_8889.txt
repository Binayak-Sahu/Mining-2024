Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN2104 : Mine Surveying { 3-0-0 / 3}
Subject Nature :
 Theory
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
Module 1: Surveying definition, importance of mine surveying, types of survey and principles 
 [2 L]
Module 2: Traditional Surveying Methods 
Linear and angular measurement techniques, Linear measurement in obstacles, Field work & Plotting of maps. [5 L]
Module 3: Traversing: Traverse survey using Compass and Theodolite. Open and closed traverse, Concept of bearing, traverse calculations, error corrections and adjustments. [5 L]
Module 4: Levelling [4 L]
Types of levels survey for RL calculations of points, Concept of contour, uses of contouring, Methods of contour plotting. 
Module 5: Correlation Survey [3 L]
Correlation of surface and underground surveys: Verticality of shafts, shaft depth measurement, Direct traversing in inclined shaft, correlation in vertical shaft –single and two shafts .
Module 6: Setting out curves – surface and underground. [3 L]
Module 7: Computation of areas and volumes of earthworks [4 L]
Module 8: GPS: Theory and principles of GPS & DGPS Surveying and its applications in mines.
 [3 L]
Module 9: Digital Mine Plan Preparation: Tools for Digital mine map preparation. Photogrammetry Survey, Basics of GIS, Projection systems of maps. [5 L]
Module 10: Statutory requirements of mine Surveying: General requirements about mine plans and
sections, Types of plans and sections. [2 L]
 Course Objective
 1 .
Make understanding the role of Mine survey course in mine operations
 2 .
Enable to learn the method of conducting the mine survey
 3 .
Enable to prepare and understand the mine maps
 4 .
Learn about the modern mine surveying tools
 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO1: Understanding basic theory of basic tools and techniques of mine survey.
CO2: Develop necessary skills to conduct mine survey using different tools and technique. 
CO3: Develop analytical skills for solving mine survey problems. 
CO4: Develop skills for handling advanced survey technique for digital mine map preparation.
CO5: Understanding the applications of Drone Survey for mining industry.
