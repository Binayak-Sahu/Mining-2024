Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN3202 : Mine Legislation and Safety { 3-0-0 / 3}
Subject Nature :
 Theory
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
Module1: Introduction: Mine Legislation, General principles of Mining Law, Principal Provisions of Mines Act, Mines, and Minerals (Regulation and Development) Act. [5 hours]
Module 2: Coal Mines Regulation, Metalliferous Mines Regulation, Mine Rescue Rules, Indian Forest Act, National Mineral Policy [7 hours]
Module 3: Mineral Concession Rules, Mineral Conservation Rules, Mines Rules 1955, Electricity Rules, Industrial Disputes Act. 1947. [6 hours]
Module 4: Mining Accidents: Their causes and prevention, accident statistics, rates of accidents, the relation between accidents and job efficiency, accident investigation, accident reports, and cost of accidents. [5 hours]
Module 5: Mine Safety, Safety Risk Assessment, and Management, Safety Audit, Occupational health and safety in mines. [6 hours]
Module 6: Mine Safety Management Systems, Safety Education, and Training, Vocational Training Rules. [5 hours]
 Course Objective
 1 .
The students are made conversant with legal requirements and safety aspects of mining
 2 .
The students are made conversant with Coal and Metal Mining Rules and Regulations
 3 .
The students are made conversant with Mines Act and Mines Rules applicable to Indian Mines
 4 .
The students are made conversant with mine accidents and safety aspects of mining
 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO1: To introduce General principles of Mining Law including various acts, rules, regulations, and bylaws.
CO2: To learn procedures for accident investigation and preparation of accident reports.
CO3: To understand Safety risk assessment principles and the Safety management system in mines
