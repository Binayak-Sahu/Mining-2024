Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN5023 : Computer Application in Mining Laboratory { 0-0-3 / 2}
Subject Nature :
 Practical
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
1 MATLAB: Basic programming exercises, Flowcharts and Algo
Module 2 : 
2 LEM: Design a Slope with given dimensions. Put material properties. Find the FOS and depth of Critical failure circle
Module 3 : 
3 LEM: Support the slope and find optimum reinforcement by varying bolt properties. Do basic cost analysis sensitivity. 
Module 4 : 
4 FEM: Simple bar stretch simulation. Max deformation analysis against a given load.
Module 5 : 
5 FEM: See change in deformation. Sensitivity Analysis. See effect of change in load, change in length, change in EM, change in meshing
Module 6 : 
6 FEM: Compression of ground soil while dumping operation. Find maximum deformation on ground: Stages
Module 7 : 
7 FEM: Dump design based on ground soil condition. Find maximum height.
Module 8 : 
8 FEM: Slope design and calculating critical SRF. Supporting and checking difference.
Module 9 : 
9 FEM: 3D analysis of loaded bar on ANSYS
Module 10 : 
10 FVM: Fluid flow through varying cross section pipe : ANSYS
Module 11 : 
11 Ventilation Simulation Using VENTSIM Basic flow exercise
Module 12 : 
12 MATLAB: Curve fitting linear regression exercise. Custom equation, best fit, R2 and Adjusted R2
Module 13 : 
13 MATLAB: MVRA: polynomial fitting. Compare with Excel
Module 14 : 
14 MATLAB: Artificial Neural Networks(ANN) and application
Module 15 : 
15 MATLAB: Adaptive Neuro Fuzzy Inference System (ANFIS) exercise
 Course Objective
 1 .
To have a hands on experience in solving various mining Problems using computational techniques and software
 2 .
Developing aptitude to transform various mining problem and other practical engineering problems and challenges into computationally viable workflow and solutions
 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO 1. Understand the fundamental concepts of computational programming, flowchart development, and algorithm design using MATLAB for solving mining-related problems. 
 2 .
CO 2. Apply numerical methods such as Limit Equilibrium Method (LEM), Finite Element Method (FEM), and Finite Volume Method (FVM) to analyze slope stability, soil deformation, and fluid flow in mining applications. 
 3 .
CO 3. Analyze the effects of different material properties, boundary conditions, and external forces on slope stability, ground deformation, and ventilation performance using computational tools like ANSYS and VENTSIM. 
 4 .
CO 4. Evaluate the accuracy and efficiency of predictive modeling techniques such as regression analysis, artificial neural networks (ANN), and adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference systems (ANFIS) for mining applications. 
 5 .
CO 5. Design optimized engineering solutions for slope reinforcement, dump design, and ventilation management by integrating numerical simulations and data-driven approaches.
