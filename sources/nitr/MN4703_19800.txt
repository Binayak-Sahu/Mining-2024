Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN4703 : Computerized Mine Planning and Design Laboratory { 0-0-3 / 2}
Subject Nature :
 Practical
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
L01: Selection of mining method using UBC Modified Nicholas methods with case studies
L02: Ultimate Pit Slope limit determination using Floating cone and 2D L&G algorithm (Economic Pit Shell)
L03: Preparation, Visualization and Interpretation of borehole log data using Surpac
L04: Preparation and Visualization of geological model from bore hole log data using Surpac (Geological wireframe model)
L05: Preparation of a geological block model and estimation of solid volume, grade-tonnage curves
L06: Design of pit slopes, ramps and waste dumps using Surpac and estimation of stripping ratio
L07: Advanced Production Planning & Scheduling in open pit mines (using MineSched)
L08: Estimation of rock support (rock bolt, shotcrete, etc.) requirement using Empirical approach
L09: Design of Stope size and Crown pillar dimension for a u/g metalliferous mines using Empirical approach
L10: Use of Drone and Lidar data for preparation of DTM, contour and volumetric estimations
 Course Objective
 1 .
Understand basis of geological data preparation for Computer aided designs
 2 .
Comprehend the workflow for geological model preparations in software tools
 3 .
Understand the concept of block modelling and computerized pit and dump design
 4 .
Plan, design and undertake stability assessment of a mining method, stope design, support requirements using Empirical Approaches
 5 .
Use drone & lidar data for design of surface, contour and volumetric estimations
 Course Outcome
 1 .
Understand basis of pit design from exploration data
 2 .
Able to design an open pit mine and waste dump using mine planning software
 3 .
Understand the practice of production planning & scheduling in both surface & underground mines
