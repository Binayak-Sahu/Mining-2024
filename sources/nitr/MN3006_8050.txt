 Course Objective
 1 .
To familiarize students with the sampling, reserve calculation and mine valuation.
 2 .
To develop analytical skills for resource estimation.
 3 .
To familairaize students with the basic concepts of goestatistics.
 4 .
To understand the point and global methods for natural resource estimation.
 Course Outcome
 1 .
1. Student will be able to understand the sampling and reserve estimation technique.
2. Students will be able to analyze the real mine data for better mine planning and design.
3. Students will be able to comprehend different geostatistics techniques for resource estimation.
