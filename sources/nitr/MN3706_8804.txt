 Course Objective
 1 .
To be aware and understand the different physical and mechanical properties of rocks, soils and aggregate materials
 2 .
To identify correctly the methods and standards of testing for different geo-mechanical properties based on requirement of the problem.
 3 .
To be able to independently perform laboratory testing for determining various geo-mechanical properties of materials.
 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO 1. Understand the principles and procedures of mechanical property testing of aggregates and construction materials, 
 2 .
CO 2. Apply laboratory techniques to determine soil compaction and permeability characteristics for geotechnical engineering applications. 
 3 .
CO 3. Analyze the strength and bearing capacity of soil to assess their suitability for construction. 
 4 .
CO 4. Evaluate the quality and structural integrity of construction materials through non-destructive testing methods 
 5 .
CO 5. Design effective material testing strategies and interpret experimental results to make informed decisions for infrastructure development and geotechnical applica-tions.
