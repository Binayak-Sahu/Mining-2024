Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN3708 : Mine Modelling , Automation and Digitization Laboratory { 0-0-3 / 2}
Subject Nature :
 Practical
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
L01: Application of AI/ML based mineral exploration using remote sensing and geospatial analytics
L02: Geological modelling practices: Preparation and process of for 3D models using Implicit Modeling tools
L03: Geological modelling practices: Preparation and process of for 3D mine models
L04: 3D reconstruction of objects/model based on Structure from Motion Photogrammetry
L05: Use of Lidar data for change detection and deformation monitoring in u/g mines/tunnels
L06: Application of digital tools for mine scale 3D modelling, simulation & optimization
L07: Demonstration of AR/VR Use cases in mining operation and planning
L08: Use of Wireless sensor networks & IIoTs for real-time monitoring of mine unit operation
L09: Digital Twins: Virtual mine replicas for planning, optimization, and training
L10: Interpretation of mine instrumentation data and application of AI/ML techniques
L11: Handling, extraction, interpretation and visualization of Big Data in mining
 Course Objective
 1 .
Understand the benefit of Industry 4.0 on mining application 
 2 .
Demonstrate principles and components of major automated unit operations in mining
 3 .
Understand data collection, interpretation and visualization of various types of data in mining industry 
 Course Outcome
 1 .
Understand the fundamentals of communications in digital mining, IIoTs and Industry 4.0
 2 .
Get an overview of emerging practices in mine automation & digitalization
 3 .
Gain understating of how mining industry can be digitized mining
