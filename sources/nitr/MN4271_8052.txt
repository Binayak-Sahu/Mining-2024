Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN4271 : Environmental Engineering Laboratory { 0-0-3 / 2}
Subject Nature :
 Practical
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
1. Measurement of Illuminance by Digital Light meter (Model: Metravi 1332)
2. Measurement of Noise Level by Integrating Sound Level Meter and Model: I.CEL-283EX, II. Bruel & Kjaer 2236
3. Determination of Ignition temperature of coal by using Flammability Temperature Apparatus
4. Determination of Crossing Point Temperature of coal by C.P.T. Apparatus
5. Determination of Gross Calorific value of coal by Bomb Calorimeter.
6. Determination of Spontaneous Heating Susceptibility of coal using Differential Thermal Analyzer (DTA), Model: SHIMADZU, DTG-60/60H
7. Measurement of Respirable dust concentration by PM10 & PM 2.5 Sampler: Model: I. Envirotech APM 460NL II. Envirotech APM 550
8. Determination of Aerosol Mass concentration by Dust Track II, 8532
9. Determination of different parameters of Water using Water Testing kits
10. Determination of different parameters of Soil using Soil Testing kits
11. Audiometry Test by using digital Audiometer
12. To assess the lung functioning test using spirometer
 Course Objective
 1 .
To know the different methods of coal analysis for assessing liability to spontaneous heating and mine fires
 2 .
To analyse particulate matter concentration in air.
 3 .
Measure different parameters of water, soil, noise and illumination
 Course Outcome
 1 .
1. CO 1. Assess illuminance levels at different work places using digital lux meter.
CO 2. Determine SPL of machineries using Sound level meters and hearing acuity using digital audiometer.
CO 3.Assess spontaneous combustion liabilities of coal and understand fire risk of coals.
CO 4. Measure ambient particulate matter concentration (particulate matter: PM10 and PM2.5,) status in air.
CO 5. Analyze water and soil quality parameters in mines and know their relevance for mining applications.
