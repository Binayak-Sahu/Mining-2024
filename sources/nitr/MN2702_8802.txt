Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN2702 : Mine Surveying Laboratory { 0-0-3 / 2}
Subject Nature :
 Practical
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
1 Chain Surveying
(a) Offset measurement of objects and map preparation 3 Hours
2 Compass Surveying 
(a) Bearing of the lines (b) Traversing 3 Hours
3 Level Survey 
Differential Levelling/Profile Levelling 3 Hours
4 Reciprocal Levelling 3 Hours
5 Contour Survey 3 Hours
6 Theodolite Survey 
Horizontal angle measurement/Vertical angle measurement 3 Hours
7 Theodolite Traversing 3 Hours
8 Correlation Survey 3 Hours
9 Curve Settings 3 Hours
10 Survey using GPS/EDM/Total Station 3 Hours
11 Preparation of Mine Plans in GIS Software
(a) Digitization
(b) Geo-reference of maps 3 Hours
 Course Objective
 1 .
To impart knowledge of operating different survey instruments and its accessories
 2 .
To impart knowledge of conducting various types of mine survey.
 3 .
To impart knowledge of mine map preparation
 4 .
To impart knowledge about advanced survey tools and techniques
 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO1: Students will be able to operate different survey equipment.
CO2: Students will be able to carry out the survey work in mines for different purposes.
CO3: Students will be able to prepare and manage the mine maps.
CO4: Develop skills to understand the tools and techniques of advanced surveying.
