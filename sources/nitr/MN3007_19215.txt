Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN3007 : Introduction to Numerical Modelling { 3-0-0 / 3}
Subject Nature :
 Theory
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
Introduction to Mechanics, Stress-Strain, Behaviour of materials, Introduction to simulations and scientific computing tools.
Module 2 : 
Introduction and Basics: Basics of Numerical Methods, Simulations and scientific computing introduction, Matrix algebra and solution techniques, Interpolation and Approximation, Differentiation and Integration, Ordinary differential equation (ODE), Partial-differential equations (PDE).
Module 3 : 
Intro & concepts of FDM, Mathematical concepts, Initial value (IVP) & Boundary value problem (BVP), Boundary Conditions, Finite difference approximation. 
Module 4 : 
Intro & concepts of FEM, Mathematical concepts, Direct Method, Weighted Integral & Weak Formulation, 1-D BVP problems of 2nd order, Applications, Errors & convergence, Explicit and implicit method, Nodes and Elements.
Module 5 : 
Application of Methods: Mining, civil and mechanical application of Finite Difference Method (FDM), Finite Element Method (FEM), Finite Volume Method (FVM), Boundary Element Method (BEM), and Discrete Element Method (DEM).
 Course Objective
 1 .
To understand the basic concepts of engineering mechanics.
 2 .
Clarity in principles and engineering application of numerical methods.
 3 .
Learn to approximate a function using various interpolation techniques, numerical integration.
 4 .
Clarity in principles and engineering application of numerical methods
 5 .
Develop an understanding of the principles of FDM and FEM.
 Course Outcome
 1 .
Understand the concept and principles of stress-strain, constitutive behavior materials.
 2 .
Remember the concept of matrices and apply for solving linear simultaneous equations.
 3 .
To apply, analyze, and implement various methods for solving linear simultaneous equations.
 4 .
Derive appropriate numerical methods to solve linear systems of equations.
 5 .
To apply numerical methods to real-world engineering problems.
