Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN5027 : Novel and innovative mining practices { 3-0-0 / 3}
Subject Nature :
 Theory
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
Innovative Mining Technology in Opencast (High wall mining, Boundary pillar mining) and Underground mines (Sub-level caving, Block caving, Top-coal caving). Mining of narrow vein deposits. 
Digitization & Digital Transformation in Mining, Robotics, Tele-monitoring in Mines, Short Interval Control (SIC), Interoperability, Innovations in Instrumentation and Communication System, Internet of Things (IoTs), Use of Virtual Reality and Mixed Reality
Application of Drones, Laser Scanner for surveying, mapping and monitoring in Mines. Tools for Digital Outcrop Mapping and Modelling, Digital rockmass characterization.
Automation in Mining, Digital Twining, Battery & Electric vehicles, Measurement While Drilling (MWD), Emerging technologies in Remote Sensing & GIS, De-carbonization and Green-mining.
Extra-Terrestrial Mining, Deep Sea Mining, Dredging, Mining in the Arctic. Bioleaching and Heap Leaching, Clean Coal Technology, Oil Sand mining, Tailings Re-mining
Emerging rock excavation approaches: Road header, Tunnel Boring machine, Raise borer, Blast-free mining.
 Course Objective
 1 .
Understand unique mining methods and practices
 2 .
To acquire the knowledge about mining in challenging environment
 3 .
Understand application latest techniques and tools in mining
 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO1: To understand mining of unique and challenging ore bodies
CO2: To study innovative mining practices in surface and underground mining operation
CO3: Understand of mining of unconventional mining methods
CO4: Comprehend use of digitalization and automations in mining industry
CO5: Recognize the use of Industry 6.0 and beyond in mines
