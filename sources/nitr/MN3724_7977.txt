 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
List of experiments to be conducted 
1. Study of instruments for monitoring convergence in mines. ( 2 Hours)
2. Study of instruments for monitoring stress in underground workings. ( 2 Hours)
3. Study of instruments for monitoring load on supports. ( 2 Hours)
4. Study of instrumentation for monitoring ground behavior around Longwall workings. ( 2 Hours) 
5. Study of instruments for slope monitoring in opencast mines. ( 2 Hours)
6. Study of instruments for roof fall monitoring in mines. ( 2 Hours)
7. Study of instruments for monitoring bed separation in mines( 2 Hours)
8. Study of instruments for monitoring ground vibrations due to blasting. ( 2 Hours)
9. Study of instruments for strata behavior monitoring in thick seams. ( 2 Hours)
10. Study of instrumentation for monitoring ground behavior in metal mines ( 2 Hours)

 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO1: Identify and describe various instruments used for monitoring ground deformation parameters in underground mines, including convergence and bed separation.
CO2: Explain the principles of operation and application of instruments employed for measuring stress and load distribution in underground workings and on support systems.
CO3: Analyze the instrumentation techniques specifically used for monitoring ground behavior and stability in Longwall mining operations and thick seam mining environments.
CO4: Select and apply appropriate instrumentation for monitoring potential hazards in both underground and opencast mines, such as roof falls and slope instability.
CO5: Evaluate the use of instruments for assessing the impact of mining activities on the surrounding ground, including the monitoring of ground vibrations caused by blasting.
