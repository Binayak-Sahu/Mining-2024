Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN2172 : Rock Mechanics Laboratory { 0-0-3 / 2}
Subject Nature :
 Practical
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
Preparation of rock specimen for determination of physio-mechanical properties of rock
Module 2 : 
 Determination of Durability Index of Rock and coal sample by Slake Durability Apparatus
Module 3 : 
Determine the strength index of rock and coal aggregates by Protodykonov apparatus 
Module 4 : 
Evaluation of Impact strength of Rock aggregates by Impact Strength apparatus
Module 5 : 
Determination of Uni-axial compressive strength of rock materials by Compression M/C
Module 6 : 
Determine the Uni-axial Tensile strength of rock specimen by Indirect method (Brazilian test)
Module 7 : 
Determination of Point load strength Index of rock specimen (Cylindrical & Irregular Specimen) 
Module 8 : 
Evaluation of shear strength parameter of Soil by direct shear –test apparatus 
Module 9 : 
Determination of modulus of elasticity and passions ratio of rock samples
Module 10 : 
Determination of cohesion and angle of Internal friction by tri-axial test
Module 11 : 
Determination of index parameter of rock mass using Schmidt hammer test
 Course Objective
 1 .
To select the right sample and prepare it according to the tests needed
 2 .
To identify the type of machines needed for each test and operate those machine with minimum assistance/guidance
 3 .
To decide the type of tests needed to obtain the right parameters of rocks
 4 .
To carry out determination of different properties of rocks
 5 .
To interpret the results and analyse those for engineering design purpose
 Course Outcome
 1 .
 To be able to select the source of rock/soil samples for further investigation 
 2 .
To be able to prepare and perform tests for index properties of soil/rock as durability and surface properties
 3 .
To able to prepare and perform tests for mechanical properties of soil/rock as compressive, tensile and shear parameters
 4 .
 To be able to prepare, perform and determine three-dimensional stress properties 
 5 .
 To perform and predict rock properties from simple field tests apparatus
