Subject {L-T-P / C} :
 MN4223 : Eco-friendly Mining { 3-0-0 / 3}
Subject Nature :
 Theory
 Syllabus
Module 1 : 
Overview: Basic concept of eco-friendly mining. Selection of eco-friendly equipment and exploitation operations, Environmental Parameters: 
Water quality – physical, chemical, biological, criteria and standards. Classification and chemistry of major air pollutants, Soil chemistry – nature and importance of soil, soil properties, soil amendments
Waste Management: Wastewater management – sources characteristics, techniques of treatment. Acid mine drainage – occurrence, effects and treatment techniques Solid waste management for mine spoils, 
Mine Reclamation & Mine Closure: Mine Reclamation strategies, Principles, planning, financial provisions, implementation, and standards for closure criteria, systems approach for mine closure and development of closure plan, Socio-economic Aspects of Mining
 Course Objective
 1 .
To make the students familiar with the concepts of eco friendly mining practices and their application
 2 .
To understand the impact of mining on water and soil, and the remediation techniques.
 3 .
Understand the process of rehabilitation and mine closure and its implementation.
 Course Outcome
 1 .
CO1: Understand the basic concepts of eco-friendly mining and its application.
CO2: Select and adopt appropriate technologies for better environmental conditions in mines.
CO3: Estimate the extent of environmental impact due to mining and decide the management options.
CO4: Understand the process of rehabilitation and mine closure and its implementation.
