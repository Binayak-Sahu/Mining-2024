## Disagreements and how they were resolved
- **UEL:** NIOSH IC 9486 gives 15 %; Zlochower & Green measured 15.8 % (120-L) and 15.0 % (flammability tube). The flow uses the handbook's 5–15 % and says lab vessels measure slightly wider.
- **Nose / LOC:** the handbook's rule of thumb is 12 % O₂; the lab LOC is 11.1–12.0 % depending on the apparatus. The flow says "about 12 %, lab tests 11–12 %". Academic sources agree within the apparatus spread, and no statute value is involved.
