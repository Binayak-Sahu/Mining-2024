## Disagreements
- **Unit weight:** Hoek's typical 0.027 MN/m³ vs STRATA's existing 0.025 MPa/m field rule (ρ = 2.5 t/m³). This is not a conflict, since both are "typical" values. The flow quotes Hoek's number, and the lesson's sims keep their own density slider.
